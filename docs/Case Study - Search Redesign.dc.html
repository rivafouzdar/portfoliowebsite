Case study 03 Buyer UX · Conversion

Search & product discovery on AWS Marketplace

Search is the busiest page in AWS Marketplace, but its results were a long, text-heavy list. I redesigned it as a grid of scannable product cards, with actions right on each card, to get more buyers from search to product pages and, ultimately, to increase conversion.

ROLELead Senior UX Designer
TEAMPM, SDM, search engineering, data science
TIMELINEApr – Jun 2026
STATUSLive on AWS Marketplace
01 — The problem

A wall of text between buyers and the product they need

The old results page gave each listing a full-width row: a logo, a title link, and a long, cut-off description. Only three or four results fit on screen, similar listings from the same seller were hard to tell apart, and the details buyers use to decide (pricing model, free trial, delivery method) were hidden until they clicked through.

The only action was the title link. Buyers had to open each product page to see if it was worth a closer look, so many left search without opening any.

03 — Process

Tested on the highest-traffic page

Because search gets so much traffic, even small changes to the layout affect a huge number of buyers. I explored several card layouts and grid densities, ran usability sessions with enterprise buyers, and worked with data science on an A/B test. We then rolled the change out gradually over about a month, watching engagement at each step before launching to everyone.
