## Projects (Portfolio Case Studies)

### 1. Marketplace in Claude and ChatGPT (MCP Connector)
**What it is:** An agentic experience that surfaces AWS Marketplace discovery and transactional actions directly inside Claude and ChatGPT, powered by an MCP server that engineering built and I designed the end-to-end experience for.

**My role:** Designed the full UX for the connector — how users discover, search, and take action inside the AI chat context, how Marketplace content surfaces in responses, and how the experience stays consistent with both Cloudscape (AWS's design system) and the host AI product's patterns.

**Why it matters:** It extends Marketplace beyond the browser into where enterprise buyers are already working. It required designing for a new interaction paradigm (agentic, conversational, embedded) with no established playbook.

**Skills:** Agentic experience design, MCP/connector design, cross-platform UX, design systems

---

### 2. AI-Powered Search Redesign (AWS Marketplace)
**What it is:** A redesign of search and discovery on AWS Marketplace, built on top of our launched agentic chat experience (an internal AI assistant that orchestrates a suite of internal agents).

**My role:** Leading end-to-end design for how AI changes the search experience — query understanding, relevance signals, results presentation, and discovery flows for enterprise buyers evaluating software.

**Why it matters:** Search is the highest-traffic surface on Marketplace. Translating agentic capabilities into a search experience that still feels trustworthy and efficient for enterprise buyers is a hard design problem.

**Skills:** AI product design, search and discovery UX, enterprise UX, design-to-production workflow
